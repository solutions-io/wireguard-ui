Help

What it does
The server only forwards traffic from VPN clients that these rules allow, and rejects the rest. Clients keep their configuration: nobody has to import anything.
Allowed destinations
Addresses or networks that clients may reach on every port, such as our own servers. For example 49.13.17.136 or 10.0.0.0/8.
Allowed ports
Ports that clients may use towards every other address, for example 443 for websites or a range such as 8000-8100. Keep 53 when clients use the DNS servers from the global settings.
Always allowed
Replies to allowed connections, and ping.
On the server
The rules are in the nftables table inet wireguard_ui and in the file wireguard-ui-firewall.nft next to the WireGuard config. If the table disappears, for example after reloading nftables, Wireguard UI loads it again within 30 seconds.

Add several destinations

One destination per line: the address or network, optionally followed by a description.
